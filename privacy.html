Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect through cedar-chiro.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website — it does not describe the protected health information handled inside our practice or our patient records system, which are governed separately (see "Health information" below).

Last updated: August 2026.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you use the appointment-request or contact form on this site, we ask for details such as your name, phone number, email address, and a brief reason for your visit so our front desk can follow up. If you don't submit a form, we don't collect this information.

Like most websites, our host may automatically log basic technical data (such as browser type, approximate location, and pages visited) to keep the site secure and running well. This data is not used to personally identify you.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule or confirm an appointment.
  • To answer questions you send us and provide the care information you asked about.
  • To keep the website secure, reliable, and easy to use.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Online booking & the patient portal

When you book online or use the patient portal, you're taken to our scheduling and records provider (Jane). Any information you enter there is handled under Jane's own privacy and security practices, not this website's. We encourage you to review their policies when you create an account.

Health information

Please don't include detailed medical history or sensitive health information in the website contact form. A short reason for your visit is all we need to route your request. Protected health information you share once you become a patient is kept confidential and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws, separate from this website.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use limited cookies or analytics to understand how visitors use our pages so we can improve them. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

Links to other sites

Our site links to outside services such as our booking system, Google, and Facebook. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and this policy doesn't cover them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete the information you submitted through this website. Just reach out using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? We're happy to help.
