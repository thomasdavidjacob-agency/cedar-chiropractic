Chiropractic Care After a Car Accident: Why the First Days Matter

By the Cedar Chiropractic Team · 6 min read · Back to blog

A car accident can rattle you even when the damage looks minor. Here in Milwaukie, and for our neighbors in Gladstone, Oregon City, Oak Grove, and across the Portland area, one of the most common things we hear is, "I felt fine at the scene, but a few days later my neck and back were killing me." That delay is a big reason the first days after a crash matter.

Why car-accident injuries are easy to miss

In the moments after a collision, your body floods with adrenaline. That surge can mask pain for hours or even days, which is why so many people decline care at the scene and only realize later that something's wrong. Whiplash and other soft-tissue injuries are sneaky that way. The stiffness, headaches, and radiating pain often set in 24 to 72 hours after impact, once the adrenaline fades and inflammation builds.

Getting evaluated early does two things. It can catch problems before they settle in, and it creates a clear record that they started with the crash.

How chiropractic care may help after an auto injury

Chiropractic care is non-surgical and drug-free, and it's a common choice for the strains, sprains, and stiff joints that follow a collision. At Cedar Chiropractic & Auto Injury, Dr. Fred Seater starts with an exam to work out what was injured, then builds a plan that may include:

  • Gentle spinal adjustments to help joints that locked up on impact move normally again.
  • Cervical and lumbar traction to ease pressure on discs and nerves, which may help when a crash aggravates a disc.
  • Therapeutic ultrasound and electrical muscle stimulation (E-stim) to help calm muscle spasm and support soft-tissue healing.
  • Rehabilitation exercises to rebuild strength and stability, which may help the area recover and make flare-ups less likely.

The aim is to help your body recover properly, not just take the edge off, so a "minor" accident is less likely to drag on for months.

You may not pay anything out of pocket to get started

Oregon requires personal injury protection (PIP) coverage on auto insurance policies. PIP typically pays for reasonable and necessary medical care after a crash, often regardless of who was at fault, so many patients start with little or no upfront cost. We verify your benefits and bill your auto insurer directly (see the insurance we accept), so you can focus on healing instead of paperwork. Your own policy governs what's covered, so ask your agent or insurer, and treat this as general information, not legal advice. If an attorney is involved, we're happy to coordinate with them as well.

Records that support your recovery and your claim

Careful, consistent records do more than track your progress. If you're working through an insurance or personal-injury claim, thorough documentation of your injuries, treatment, and recovery timeline often matters. We keep detailed notes from your first visit forward and can communicate directly with your insurer or attorney when needed.

What to do in the first days after a crash

Even if you feel okay, it's worth getting checked, because the injuries that surface later are easy to wave off.
