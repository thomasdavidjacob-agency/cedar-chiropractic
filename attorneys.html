Documentation

What goes in the chart

Every auto-injury patient starts with a full evaluation, and the record is kept to the same standard from the first visit on.

Objective exam findings

A thorough initial evaluation with objective, repeatable findings, recorded alongside the patient's own description of their symptoms.

Measured range of motion

Range of motion is measured and written into the chart, so the record shows numbers as well as the patient's report.

A treatment rationale

The plan of care is written down with the reasons for it, tied to what the exam found. When findings call for it, we refer out for imaging or a specialist evaluation.

Records and narrative reports on request

Visit notes, exam findings and billing statements, plus a narrative report summarizing care when one is requested, released with the patient's written permission.

What the records are, and aren't. Our records say what we saw and what we did. We don't write our notes to help a claim along, and we don't comment on what a case is worth, who was at fault, or how it should resolve. That belongs to you.

Referrals

How a client referral works

Your client doesn't need a referral, a police report, or paperwork from your office to start care.

1

Your client reaches us

They call us at (503) 653-2232, text our front desk from any page of this site, or book through our online scheduler on Jane. Whatever is easiest for them.

2

We open the PIP claim

We open the claim with your client's auto carrier and bill PIP directly. A claim number helps, but the insurer's name is enough to start.

3

We coordinate with your office

Have your client bring your office's contact information to the first visit, or send it through the form at cedar-chiro.com/attorneys, so records go to the right place without back and forth.

If a patient asks about finding an attorney, we can point them toward reputable local firms, with no obligation, and we take no referral fee.

Records & Reports

Requesting records or a narrative report

  1. Open the request. Use the form at cedar-chiro.com/attorneys and choose Records request or Report request, or call our front desk.
  2. Send the patient's written authorization. We release records with the patient's written permission. Our front desk will tell you how to get the signed authorization to us.
  3. Tell us what you need. Visit notes and exam findings, billing statements, a narrative report, or some combination, and the dates of service it should cover.
  4. Give us some lead time. Records and reports take a little time to put together, and how long depends on what's being asked for. If you need something by a certain date, tell us early rather than the day before.

Please don't put medical details, dates of birth, or claim numbers in the website form. Use it to open the request and we'll follow up by phone.

Billing

The order care is billed in

We handle the billing in this order, so your client isn't fronting money for care while the claim is open.

1. PIP

First stop. We open the claim with the patient's auto carrier and bill them directly, with no copay and no invoice to the patient.

2. Health insurance or MedPay

If PIP limits run out before the patient is finished healing, we move to their medical insurance or MedPay coverage and keep care going.

3. Attorney lien

When a case is pending, we can accept a lien and be paid out of the settlement — so treatment isn't on hold waiting for the claim to resolve.

Each patient's benefits and limits are set by their own policy. The policy governs, and the insurer or agent can confirm the specifics. Nothing on this page is legal advice.
