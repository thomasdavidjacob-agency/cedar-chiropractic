Our Specialty

Auto & Personal Injury Care

If you've been hurt in a car accident, we're the team to see. Dr. Fred evaluates and treats accident injuries, documents everything thoroughly for your claim, and coordinates with your attorney and insurer — while billing PIP / MedPay directly, so there's little or nothing out of pocket to get started.

  • Whiplash, neck & back injuries after a crash
  • PIP / MedPay billing & attorney-lien handling
  • Thorough documentation for your personal-injury claim
  • Using your PIP is not treated as an at-fault claim against you

What We Offer

Therapies & services

Spinal Adjustments

Precise, gentle spinal manipulation to correct misalignments, reduce nerve pressure, and restore natural movement. The foundation of chiropractic care.

Read more about spinal adjustments →

Spinal Decompression

A non-surgical, drug-free therapy that gently stretches the spine to relieve pressure on discs and nerves — ideal for sciatica and herniated discs.

Read more about spinal decompression →

Cervical & Lumbar Traction

Cervical traction: gentle, targeted traction that eases pressure on the cervical discs and nerves — an effective non-surgical option for neck pain and cervical disc herniations.

Lumbar traction: decompressive traction for the lower back that relieves pressure on lumbar discs, calming sciatica and disc-related pain without surgery.

Read more about traction →

Ultrasound & E-Stim

Therapeutic ultrasound: deep sound-wave therapy that boosts circulation and speeds healing in strained muscles, tendons, and other soft tissue.

Electrical stimulation (E-Stim): gentle electrical muscle stimulation that reduces pain, relaxes spasms, and supports recovery after an injury.

Read more about ultrasound & e-stim →

Massage & Soft Tissue

Therapeutic massage and myofascial release that complement adjustments, easing muscle tension and speeding recovery.

Read more about massage & soft tissue →

Rehab Exercises

Personalized corrective and strengthening exercises that restore stability, speed recovery, and help keep your pain from coming back.
