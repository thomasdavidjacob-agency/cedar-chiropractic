Our Services

Comprehensive care, tailored to you

Whether you're recovering from an injury or investing in lifelong wellness, we have a proven, gentle approach for you.

Home / Services

Spinal Adjustments

Precise, gentle spinal manipulation to correct misalignments, reduce nerve pressure, and restore natural movement. The foundation of chiropractic care.

Spinal Decompression

A non-surgical, drug-free therapy that gently stretches the spine to relieve pressure on discs and nerves — ideal for sciatica and herniated discs.

Sports & Injury Rehab

Sport-specific rehabilitation combining adjustments, soft-tissue therapy, and corrective exercise to get athletes back to peak performance safely.

Prenatal & Pediatric

Comfortable, low-force techniques that support expecting mothers through pregnancy and help children stay healthy as they grow.

Headache & Migraine Relief

Targeted care for tension headaches and migraines rooted in neck and upper-back dysfunction — reducing frequency and intensity naturally.

Auto & Work Injury

Whiplash and workplace-injury care with complete documentation for insurance and legal claims — so you can focus on healing.

Massage & Soft Tissue

Therapeutic massage and myofascial release that complement adjustments, easing muscle tension and speeding recovery.

Posture & Ergonomics

Personalized posture assessments and workspace guidance to prevent the desk-bound pain that brings so many patients in.

Wellness & Maintenance

Ongoing tune-ups that keep you moving well, prevent flare-ups, and support your long-term health goals.
