Meet Dr. Fred Seater

A different kind of chiropractic experience

Dr. Fred Seater has spent years helping Milwaukie families move without pain. He founded Cedar Chiropractic & Auto Injury to combine the best of hands-on chiropractic tradition with modern, evidence-based rehabilitation — with a genuine focus on caring for people hurt in auto and personal-injury accidents.

What sets Cedar apart is simple: time. Dr. Fred believes no one should feel rushed through their care. He takes the time to understand your story, examine you carefully, and build a plan that fits your life — not a one-size-fits-all package.

  • Doctor of Chiropractic (D.C.)
  • Gentle, hands-on adjusting for every age and comfort level
  • Experienced in auto-accident & personal-injury care
