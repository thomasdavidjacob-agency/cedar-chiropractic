Meet Dr. Fred

A different kind of chiropractic experience

Dr. Fred has spent more than 15 years helping Portlanders move without pain. After earning his Doctor of Chiropractic degree, he founded Cedar Chiropractic Physicians with a mission to combine the best of hands-on chiropractic tradition with modern, evidence-based rehabilitation.

What sets Cedar apart is simple: time. Dr. Fred believes no one should feel rushed through their care. He takes the time to understand your story, examine you carefully, and build a plan that fits your life — not a one-size-fits-all package.

  • Doctor of Chiropractic (D.C.)
  • Certified in spinal decompression & sports rehabilitation
  • Member, Oregon Chiropractic Association
