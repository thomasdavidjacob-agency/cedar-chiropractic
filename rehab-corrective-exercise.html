Rehab & Corrective Exercise

Personalized exercises that restore strength and stability — and help keep your pain from coming back.

What rehab and corrective exercise is

Hands-on care can ease pain, but the muscles that support your spine and joints decide how well you hold that progress. Rehab and corrective exercise are personalized movements chosen to rebuild strength, restore stability, and correct the patterns that keep putting stress on the same spot.

It’s the “rebuild how you move” and “keep you steady” part of our approach — the stage that helps relief last.

What it’s commonly used for

  • Recovery after injury — rebuilding strength once the acute pain has settled
  • Work injuries — on-the-job strains and repetitive-motion problems
  • Preventing flare-ups — strengthening the areas that tend to give out

What a visit feels like

We start from your exam and your goals, then show you a small number of exercises, watch your form, and adjust until each one feels right. Exercises start simple and progress as you get stronger. You’ll usually get a short routine to do between visits, and we’ll check it and update it as you improve. Nothing should cause sharp pain — if it does, we change it.

Who it may need adapting for

Almost everyone can do some form of corrective exercise, but what’s right depends on your injury, your health, and how far along you are in recovery. Certain movements may be off the table for a while after a recent injury or surgery, or with some medical conditions. We tailor every program, and we’ll coordinate with your physician when that makes sense.

How it fits with other therapies

Exercise usually builds on the relief that hands-on care provides:

  • Spinal AdjustmentsRestores joint motion so exercise feels easier
  • Spinal DecompressionRelieves disc pressure before strengthening
  • Ultrasound & E-StimSupports healing tissue early in recovery

Common Questions

Rehab & Corrective Exercise: questions we hear

Will I have to do exercises at home?

Usually, yes — a short routine between visits makes a real difference. We keep it practical, show you each movement, and check your form at your next appointment.

Can rehab help if I was hurt at work?

Yes. We treat on-the-job strains and repetitive-motion injuries, and we document and bill workers’-compensation claims directly.

What if an exercise hurts?

Tell us. Exercises shouldn’t cause sharp pain. We’ll modify the movement or swap it for something that suits where you are in recovery.
