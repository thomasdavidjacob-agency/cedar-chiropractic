Common Questions

Good to know before you come in

Do I need a referral to see a chiropractor?

No — you can book directly with us, no doctor's referral required. If your insurance has specific requirements, we'll help you sort that out.

Is the first adjustment painful?

Most patients feel relief, not pain. Dr. Fred uses gentle techniques and always adjusts his approach to your comfort level. You're in control the entire time.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing you can move in is ideal. Avoid restrictive belts or heavy jewelry if possible.

How long is the first visit?

Plan for about 45–60 minutes. This gives us time for a proper consultation, exam, and your first treatment if appropriate.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. After your exam, Dr. Fred will give you an honest estimate — we never lock you into long, unnecessary packages.
