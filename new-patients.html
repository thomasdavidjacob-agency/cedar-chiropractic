Common Questions

Good to know before you come in

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we're always happy to welcome new patients. Request an appointment online or call us at (503) 653-2232.

Do I need a referral?

No referral needed — you can book directly with us. If your insurance plan has specific requirements, we'll help you sort them out.

How soon could I be seen?

We do our best to see new patients as soon as possible — often within the same week. Call us and we'll find the earliest opening that works for you.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major medical insurance and handle the billing for you — including auto-accident (PIP) and workers'-compensation claims. Not sure if you're covered? Call us and we'll verify your benefits for free.

What if I don't have insurance?

No problem. We offer transparent, affordable self-pay rates and will walk you through your options before any care begins.

How much will this cost me?

Your cost depends on your insurance and the care you need. We're upfront about pricing before you commit, verify your benefits for free, and offer affordable self-pay rates if you're uninsured. Call us for an estimate.

Does Dr. Fred treat my type of issue or injury?

Dr. Fred cares for a wide range of concerns — back and neck pain, headaches and migraines, sciatica, auto-accident and work injuries, sports injuries, and general wellness. If you're not sure we're the right fit, call us; we'll give you an honest answer and point you in the right direction if we're not.

Do I have to be adjusted, or can we just discuss my options first?

You're always in control. Your first visit is a consultation and exam — we'll talk through what's going on and your options, and we never adjust you without your consent. Many patients start with a conversation and a plan.

Is the first adjustment painful?

Most patients feel relief, not pain. Dr. Fred uses gentle techniques and adapts his approach to your comfort level — you're in control the entire time.

Does Dr. Fred use an instrument or activator (a "clicker")?

Dr. Fred offers a range of techniques and can use gentle, low-force instrument adjusting — such as an Activator — for patients who prefer a lighter touch or aren't comfortable with manual adjustments. Let us know your preference and we'll tailor your care.

Does Dr. Fred use a drop table?

Yes — our office uses a drop-piece table, which adds a gentle, controlled assist to certain adjustments. Dr. Fred will choose the technique that best fits your body and comfort.

What chiropractic technique or methodology does Dr. Fred follow?

Dr. Fred practices evidence-based, whole-person chiropractic — combining hands-on adjustments with gentler, low-force methods and modern rehabilitation, tailored to each patient rather than a one-size-fits-all technique.

How long is a typical appointment?

Your first visit (consultation and exam) usually takes about 45–60 minutes. Follow-up visits are typically shorter — often around 15–20 minutes.

Can you get a copy of my imaging (X-rays or MRI)?

Yes. If you've had X-rays, an MRI, or other imaging, we can help you obtain copies and will review them as part of your care. Bring them along if you already have them.

Can you get my records from my previous chiropractor?

Yes — with your written authorization, we can request your records from a previous provider. Just let us know and we'll send you a simple release form.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing you can move in is ideal. Avoid restrictive belts or heavy jewelry if you can.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on your condition and goals. After your exam, Dr. Fred will give you an honest estimate — we never lock you into long, unnecessary packages.
